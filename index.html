A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

All dressed up.
All questions answered.

A little clarity before the big day.

What does “one style, one guest” mean?

A style and color can be reserved once within a wedding room. This classroom preview keeps reservations on your device only, so another device will not see your changes. A live service would need shared inventory. Guests renting elsewhere may have the same dress.

How does the four-day rental work?

The sample offer delivers your dress two days before the wedding. Return it using the included label at the end of the four-day rental. Your exact delivery and return dates appear before you confirm.

How do I choose my size?

Open a look to see its size guidance and choose an available size. Measurements in this prototype are illustrative; a real service would use verified measurements for each garment and offer help before you book.

Will this website take a payment?

No. This is an interactive classroom MVP. The dresses, weddings, prices, and reserved examples illustrate the proposed service. Demo checkout never asks for card details or charges you, and confirmation does not place a real order.

Can I change or cancel my selection?

Yes. Open “My looks,” choose Reservations, and cancel your demo reservation to make the style available again on this device. Favorites and reservations are separate for each sample wedding.
